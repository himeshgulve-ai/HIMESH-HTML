ZAMATO

Zomato is one of India’s leading online food delivery and restaurant discovery platforms. It was founded in 2008 and helps users order food, explore restaurants, read reviews, and check menus easily through its mobile app and website. The company operates in many cities across India and other countries. Zomato connects customers, restaurants, and delivery partners through technology, making food ordering fast and convenient. It is known for its red branding, quick delivery services, and wide variety of food options. Over the years, Zomato has become a popular name in the online food industry.

TO ORDER FOOD

Visit Zomato
  1. Open the Zomato website or app.

  3. Browse through the restaurant options and select your desired food items.
  4. Add the items to your cart and proceed to checkout.
  5. Provide your delivery address and payment details.
